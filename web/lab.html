Not connected

Talk to your IQ3 directly.

  1. Turn on the IQ3's Bluetooth. Press the up and down buttons together 5 times within 4 seconds. The control button flashes blue 3 times when Bluetooth comes on. If it flashes red, you just turned it off, so do it again.
  2. Force-stop the DaVinci app (long-press its icon, then App info, then Force stop). The IQ3 only talks to one app at a time.
  3. Tap Connect. Your IQ3 should be the only thing in the list.

Bluetooth switches itself off when the battery is under 10%.
